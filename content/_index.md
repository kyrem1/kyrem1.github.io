I am a second-year PhD student in mathematics at [UC San Diego](https://math.ucsd.edu/), advised by [Adrian Ioana](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~aioana/). My research interests lie in operator algebras, measured group theory, and dynamics, particularly:

- deformation/rigidity for groupoid von Neumann algebras,
- $W^*$-rigidity for group von Neumann algebras,
- rigidity and Bernoullicity of algebraic actions,
- Poisson boundaries of measured groupoids.

I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Virginia, where I worked under [Benjamin Hayes](https://sites.google.com/site/benhayeshomepage/) on operator algebras and related topics. 

Before UVA, I took undergraduate and graduate mathematics courses through dual enrollment at the [University of South Florida](https://www.usf.edu/arts-sciences/departments/mathematics-statistics/index.aspx), where I was mentored by [Thomas Bieske](https://www.usf.edu/arts-sciences/departments/mathematics-statistics/people/faculty/thomas-bieske.aspx) and [Brendan Nagle](https://www.usf.edu/arts-sciences/departments/mathematics-statistics/people/faculty/brendan-nagle.aspx).
